One Person. One Coin.

A fairness-first monetary experiment.

“Bitcoin may be the vault. FairShareCoin must be the water.”

Most money systems reward position, timing, or scale. FairShareCoin asks a different question: what if every human started from the same monetary baseline, regardless of geography, class, or luck?

Powered by Proof‑of‑Being: one coin granted to every verified, living human.
No mining race. No luck. Just you.

Forget gold, dollars, or Bitcoin. Time is the only currency that matters. FairShareCoin turns your presence into monetary agency — fairly and transparently.

One person. One coin. No inflation race by design.

Spend fractions as simply as sending a fee-light digital message.

Whether you're the 100th or 10 millionth to join, your share is the same. It doesn’t matter where you live, what you earn, or when you arrive — you still get your one.

Spend it or save it, trade or gift. Or earn fractions from others by doing good work, creating real value, or being worth remembering.

FairShareCoin does not claim intrinsic value. Any value it has emerges from real human use, trust, and participation over time.

How it works (current direction)

Register → identity uniqueness path (future ICAO integration) → wallet activation → send and receive value → account recovery continuity.

Design note: annual rebalance (burn/remint)

The current model includes a burn/remint cycle designed to reduce long-term hoarding pressure and keep fairness aligned over time. This mechanism remains part of ongoing design and implementation hardening.
